What changed

  • No schedule. No times of day, no travel legs, no cost estimate, no day tabs. The app doesn't know about your hotel or the shrine you're seeing at eleven, so it can't plan a day and shouldn't act like it can.
  • Two cuts of the same sixteen places. By place for when you're standing somewhere; by type for when you know you want to buy sake and don't care which town.
  • Types are only your four. Sake shops, eat & drink, shops & makers, breweries. Nothing about shrines, gardens or waterfalls, because that isn't what this app is for.
  • Opening hours are the payoff. "Closed Sundays", "Weekends only", "Open until 19:00", "Call first" — those come off the venue pages and no general travel app has them for a ten-seat shop in a mountain village.
  • The tab is now Saved, not Trips. People save things while reading, weeks before they book anything.

The one ordering feature kept

  • Walk them in this order, within a single town. Tōkō, Zengo, Starnet, Taiseigama, Tonoike — a route through a place, not a plan for a day, and it carries one piece of reasoning: brewery last because it shuts at five.

Worth noticing

  • The empty state at the bottom of Mashiko is honest — we have no restaurant there. Better to say so than to pad it with something we haven't been to.
